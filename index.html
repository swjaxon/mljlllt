About

Melinda L. Jackson, LLLT

Melinda is a Washington State Limited Licensed Legal Technician (LLLT) based in Bellingham, WA. LLLTs are licensed by the Washington State Supreme Court to provide legal services in family law — giving you professional guidance without the cost of a full attorney.

With a focus on compassionate, practical support, Melinda helps individuals and families in Whatcom County navigate the legal process during some of life's most challenging transitions.

"My goal is to make sure you understand your rights and options — and that you're never facing the legal system alone."

What an LLLT Can Do For You

  • Review and explain legal documents
  • Prepare and file court forms
  • Advise on your rights and options
  • Help you understand court procedures
  • Assist with negotiations (outside court)
  • Support you throughout your case

Why Choose an LLLT?

Professional Legal Help,
Without Attorney Fees

Washington State's LLLT program was created to bridge the justice gap — giving people access to qualified, licensed legal help at accessible rates.

Licensed & Regulated

LLLTs are licensed by the Washington State Supreme Court and held to strict professional and ethical standards.

More Affordable

Access professional legal assistance for family law matters at significantly lower cost than hiring a full attorney.

Specialized Focus

LLLTs specialize in family law, meaning deep, focused knowledge of the exact area you need help with.

Personal Attention

Work directly with Melinda throughout your case — no passing off to paralegals or junior staff.
